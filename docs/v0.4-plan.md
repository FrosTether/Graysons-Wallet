## 2. Tone bands set mining intensity

The band the phone locks on chooses how hard it mines. The reward is the same in every band.

| Tone | Brainwave band | Mining |
|---|---|---|
| 4.0 Hz | delta | Low: 1–2 threads |
| 7.83 Hz | theta, the Schumann resonance | Medium: half the cores |
| 11.11 Hz | alpha | High: all cores but one |

- Consensus accepts resonance proofs in 3.75–4.25 Hz, 7.50–8.20 Hz and 10.80–11.40 Hz.
- Widen the detection grid from 4–12 Hz to 2–14 Hz, so 4.0 Hz isn't sitting on the edge.
- High on a phone runs hot. Back off threads when the battery temperature climbs.

## 3. Sound layer

- **Isochronic pulse** through a speaker: the mode the microphone can lock on.
- **Binaural beats** through headphones: a different tone in each ear, offset by the band frequency. The beat only exists in the listener's head, so the microphone can't lock on it. It's for listening, not mining.
- **40 Hz**: a listening layer only. It's outside the mining bands.
- **Decision needed:** if the app plays the tone through its own speaker, a phone can unlock itself with no outside source. Either allow that, since the gate is a ritual anyway, or pause the lock while the app's own sound is playing.
- No health claims for any frequency, in the app or on the site.

## 4. Rez-style block explorer

- A prototype exists as a web page. In v0.4 it becomes an Explorer tab in the app's `assets/ui`, reading the node's `/api/status` and `/api/block?h=`.
- No flashing between 3 and 30 times a second, since that range can trigger photosensitive seizures. Pulses stay slow and soft, and the explorer respects the phone's reduce-motion setting.
