Get Qoin

Qoin (QNR) is mined on phones, and only while the phone hears a tone. Install Graysons Wallet, play a tone nearby, and your phone starts mining.

Download Graysons Wallet Release notes and checksums

For Android 8.0 or newer. Your phone will ask you to allow the install, since the app isn't on the Play Store.

Mining opens Thursday 8 October at 13:37 Eastern. Mining is open. The chain started Thursday 8 October at 13:37 Eastern.

Mine with a tone

Frostoise, the miner that comes with Graysons Wallet, only works while your phone hears one of these tones. The tone sets how hard the phone works. Every block pays the same.

Pick a tone

Play it out loud from a laptop, a speaker or another phone, with the mining phone close by. Open Frostoise, allow the microphone and wait for LOCKED. Headphones won't work, because the phone has to hear it.

How to start

  1. Install Graysons Wallet.One install adds the apps that come with it: Graysons Wallet, Frostoise and MyFrost.
  2. Create a wallet.Tap Create wallet and write down your 25 words. They're the only way back into your coins, so keep them offline and never share them. Updating from an earlier build? Uninstall it first, then tap Restore and enter your 25 words. Wallets from 0.3 belong to the old chain, so make a new one.
  3. Mine.Open Frostoise, play a tone and tap Start mining. Phones on the same Wi-Fi find each other. To reach everyone else, add an always-on node. Mined coins unlock after 12 blocks, about an hour.
  4. Claim your name.Pick a .frostchain name, so people can pay you as @you instead of a long address.

The chain

Blocks
About one every 5 minutes, and never sooner than 4½ minutes after the last, so two Frost blocks for every Bitcoin Cash block.
Reward
About 263.88 QNR a block at launch, shrinking slowly as coins are mined.
Supply
Never more than 184,467,440.737 QNR. Half of it takes about 4.6 years to mine.
Unlock
Mined coins can be spent after 12 more blocks.
Mining tones
3.75–4.25, 7.50–8.20 or 10.80–11.40 Hz. Every block carries a record of the reading.
Signatures
Hash-based LMS keys (RFC 8554), built to hold up against quantum computers.
Units
QNR, divisible to 11 decimal places.
Source
Open source under GPL-3.0, on GitHub.
