QNR vs XMR

Qoin's ticker is QNR, for its CryptoNote roots: it borrows Monero's 25-word seeds and lands its supply in the same range. The rest is new. Here's how the two compare today, and what QNR could take next from Monero, Zcash and Dash.

What QNR could take next

Ideas on the table. Nothing here is decided until I finish the parameters, and anything that changes the rules ships as a planned upgrade.

From Monero

  • View keys. Let someone see a wallet's payments without being able to spend, like a crew member watching a manager's register.
  • One-time receive addresses. Monero's are built on elliptic curves, so QNR would need a hash-based version to stay quantum-safe.

From Zcash

  • Viewing keys. Private by default, provable on demand to whoever you choose.
  • Burning fees. Zcash's next upgrade, NU7, takes 60% of fees out of circulation.
  • A lockbox fund. Part of each block set aside until the community decides how to spend it.

From Dash

  • Masternodes. Always-on nodes that put up collateral (1,000 DASH) and earn part of every block, about 7% a year right now.
  • Instant locks. Groups of nodes confirm payments in seconds and lock each block against rewrites.
  • A treasury vote. Node owners vote each month on which proposals get funded.

From Steem

  • A backed dollar. Steem Backed Dollars turn into $1 of STEEM after 3.5 days, priced by the median of the witnesses' price feeds and capped at 10% of Steem's market value. A QNR version needs a real market price for QNR first.
