Temporal

A time machine for Frostchain. Seal a message for the future, or go back to any block since the chain began. Temporal runs on QNR: every seal burns 1 QNR as gas.

Seal a capsule

Write something for later. Temporal puts its fingerprint on the chain, which proves you wrote it before that block. Your words stay with you, and Temporal keeps them shut until the day you pick.

Sealed. Now burn the gas.

Send 1 QNR to the Temporal burn address with this memo. The block that carries it is your proof of when you wrote it.

Pay 1 QNR in MyFrost

On an Android phone with Graysons Wallet, this opens MyFrost with everything filled in. On a computer, scan the code with that phone.

    Fine print
    A seal is an ordinary payment, so it works on today's chain. Temporal counts a seal only if it burned at least 1 QNR and landed in a block before its opening time.
